# Congo
## Michael Crichton, 1980

**By:** Michael Crichton

In June 1979, a corporate expedition to the Congolese rainforest is annihilated near the ruins of the Lost City of Zinj. Earth Resources Technology Services dispatches a new team — a mathematical prodigy, a primatologist, a sign-language gorilla, and a mercenary guide — to recover rare Type IIb diamonds and solve the massacre. What they find is a species of grey, hybrid primates bred by an ancient civilization to guard the mines forever.

> ⚠ **Coverage note:** Herkermer Homolka appears in the 1995 film as a named character (Tim Curry) but is not a named character in the novel — the novel's rival-expedition threat is the collective unnamed Consortium. Jan Kruger's fate (death vs. survival) is ambiguous in secondary sources; he is listed as deceased based on contextual evidence from the prologue. The Kikuyu porters who accompany Munro are named collectively in some sources but no individual names were confirmed across authoritative sources and are therefore omitted.

## Setting

ERTS (Earth Resources Technology Services) is a Houston-based corporation that treats geographic and biological data as proprietary commodities, funding expeditions to secure rare industrial resources before rival consortia. The Lost City of Zinj is a fictional ruined city in the Virunga volcanic region of the eastern Congo, source of Type IIb boron-doped blue diamonds valuable as semiconductors. The grey gorillas guarding Zinj are not a natural species but a purpose-bred hybrid — gorilla, chimpanzee, and possibly human — engineered by Zinj's ancient inhabitants as living mine-guards, a fact unknown to any expedition until the novel's climax.

## ERTS (Earth Resources Technology Services)

A Houston-based corporate intelligence and resource-extraction firm that treats scientific data as a competitive weapon. Funds the second Congo expedition primarily to secure the diamond contract, subordinating the safety of its field personnel to commercial advantage.

**Dr. Karen Ross** *(protagonist)* — played by Laura Linney

![Dr. Karen Ross as played by Laura Linney](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w185/yIopIq7l7eIvjoQqnZJkpdQEvds.jpg)

A 24-year-old mathematical prodigy whose corporate loyalty and obsessive data-driven planning repeatedly endanger the team — most critically when she detonates explosive charges inside an active volcano to extract diamonds, triggering the eruption that buries Zinj. Her competence is real but instrumentalized entirely in service of ERTS's commercial contract rather than the survival of her colleagues.

**R.B. Travis** *(major)* — played by Joe Don Baker

![R.B. Travis as played by Joe Don Baker](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w185/7AEqsKLyAO3tt9bUufdirU1xXo8.jpg)

The business-driven director of ERTS who frames the entire Congo operation as a race to secure a mineral contract, treating field personnel as expendable assets. His maxim — that the easiest way to obtain information is to steal it — encapsulates the novel's critique of corporate resource extraction dressed as scientific exploration.

**Jan Kruger †** *(minor)*

A minor field operative whose night-time encounter with something 'wheezing in the darkness' near the Place of Bones serves as the novel's atmospheric prologue, establishing the grey gorillas' threat before the main expedition begins.

## Academic Primatology (San Francisco)

Peter Elliot's university research circle, focused on primate language acquisition. Drawn into the expedition through Amy's anomalous drawings, representing scientific curiosity in tension with ERTS's purely extractive agenda.

**Dr. Peter Elliot** *(protagonist)* — played by Dylan Walsh

![Dr. Peter Elliot as played by Dylan Walsh](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w185/A0XK6UEOuMKf66XpsZ1DBa9kXAR.jpg)

A San Francisco academic whose genuine affection for Amy and scientific integrity make him the expedition's moral centre, yet his professional ego — he intends to name the new hybrid species after himself — reveals the same acquisitive impulse he nominally opposes in ERTS. His ability to translate Amy's communication with the grey gorillas ultimately saves the survivors.

**Amy** *(protagonist)* — played by Misty Rosas

![Amy as played by Misty Rosas](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w185/aCa0ukemG7x3wAxtR1BM5v0BvrL.jpg)

A female gorilla with a 620-sign vocabulary whose fingerpainted dreams of Zinj initiate the entire expedition; she is both the novel's most sympathetic character and its most instrumentalized — recruited by ERTS as a navigational asset and used by Elliot as a translator with the grey gorillas. In the epilogue she is released into the wild and observed teaching her offspring sign language.

## Mercenary &amp; Field Guides

Charles Munro and his Kikuyu porters: experienced operators who navigate the Congo's political and physical dangers for pay, loyal to the expedition only as long as the contract holds.

**Charles Travis** *(major)* — played by Bruce Campbell

![Charles Travis as played by Bruce Campbell](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w185/p9335ljr7luOWsfwZSOlsIzFJKE.jpg)

A pragmatic, cynical 'white hunter' in the Allan Quatermain tradition, whose deep knowledge of the Congo's terrain and politics keeps the expedition alive through parachute drops, civil-war crossfire, and Kigani attacks. His pragmatism is the expedition's survival mechanism, though he profits personally — selling recovered diamonds to Intel in the epilogue.

## Euro-Japanese Consortium

A rival industrial coalition of German, Japanese, and Dutch interests racing ERTS to the Zinj diamond deposit. Arrives at Zinj first and is entirely destroyed by the grey gorillas before the ERTS party arrives.

**Ghost Tribe Member (Uncredited) †** *(supporting)* — played by Loren Cross

A large, well-funded coalition of German, Japanese, and Dutch industrial interests who arrive at Zinj ahead of the ERTS party and are entirely annihilated by the grey gorillas — their destroyed camp serving as the ERTS team's first direct evidence of the guardians' lethality.

## Grey Gorillas of Zinj

A purpose-bred primate species — gorilla/chimpanzee/human hybrids — engineered by the ancient Zinjians to guard the diamond mines. The true antagonists of the novel, revealed at the climax to be not a natural species but a biological weapon of territorial control.

**Gorilla** *(major)* — played by David Anthony

Not a natural species but gorilla/chimpanzee/human hybrids bred by the ancient Zinjians as territorial mine-guards — highly aggressive, partially nocturnal, and organized in troops of over a hundred. Their existence is the novel's central mystery and its climactic revelation: the 'monster' is a product of human engineering, not wilderness.

## Kigani Warriors

A hostile local tribe encountered during the expedition's flight from the erupting volcano, representing the human dangers of the Congo's civil-war-torn interior alongside the supernatural threat of the grey gorillas.

**Kigani Warriors** *(supporting)*

Cannibalistic warriors encountered during the expedition's desperate flight from the erupting Mount Mukenko, representing the human dangers of the Congo's civil-war interior as a counterpoint to the preternatural threat of the grey gorillas.
