# Tokyo Express
## Seichō Matsumoto, 1958 (tr. Jesse Kirkwood, 2022)

**By:** 松本清張

In a rocky cove at Hakata Bay, the bodies of a young couple are found — cyanide, flushed cheeks, an apparent lovers' suicide. But a dining-car receipt for one meal and a four-minute window on a railway platform convince two detectives that the deaths were murder, staged by someone with an unbreakable alibi. The novel is a 'howdunit' built on train timetables, post-war government corruption, and the quiet persistence of two very different policemen.

## Setting

Set in 1957 Japan, the novel turns on the precise schedules of the national rail network — particularly the Asakaze Express between Tokyo and Hakata. The country is in rapid post-war reconstruction, and government ministries are enmeshed in bribery scandals with industrial contractors. The alibi puzzle depends entirely on the geography of train platforms and the narrow windows of time in which passengers can be seen from one platform to another.

## Fukuoka Prefectural Police

The local force assigned to the Kashii Beach deaths; institutionally inclined to accept the double-suicide verdict and close the case quickly.

**Jūtarō Torigai** *(protagonist)*

A middle-aged, careworn provincial inspector whose shabby appearance and self-deprecating manner mask a tenacious, methodical intelligence; his institutional superiors want the case closed, yet he quietly pursues anomalies on his own time, embodying a loyalty to truth that his underfunded, hierarchical force does not reward.

## Koyuki Restaurant &amp; Yasuda's Circle

The Akasaka restaurant where Toki worked and where Yasuda cultivated his government contacts; the social world that connects victim, killer, and witnesses.

**Tetsuo Okita** *(major)* — played by Ken Takakura

![Tetsuo Okita as played by Ken Takakura](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w185/cZ0WoiLAlzanNOIoz2ZjVGEbX3m.jpg)

President of a machinery company whose rapid growth is rumoured to depend on government contracts cultivated through lavish restaurant entertaining; his seemingly watertight alibi — placing him hundreds of miles from Kashii Beach on the night of the deaths — is the central puzzle the detectives must dismantle.

**Aoki †** *(major)* — played by Sonny Chiba

![Aoki as played by Sonny Chiba](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w185/k3kHcYUBj8RskpAEDaJ0FR6ynSx.jpg)

A reserved, self-contained waitress with no obvious romantic connection to Sayama, whose apparent love-suicide with him is the premise the detectives disbelieve; her private nature means almost no one at the restaurant knew anything of her personal life, which is itself a clue.

**Yôko** *(supporting)* — played by Mitsuru Mori

A beautiful but physically confined woman whose cardiac illness keeps her isolated in a separate town, visited by Yasuda on weekends; her vulnerability and dependence on her husband are noted by Mihara as morally significant, and her situation illuminates the gendered social hierarchies Matsumoto critiques throughout.

**Waitress** *(supporting)* — played by Keiko Aikawa

![Waitress as played by Keiko Aikawa](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w185/cbOdkmY6BYudjQkELh60s8vKIkp.jpg)

Two colleagues of Toki who accompany Yasuda to Tokyo Station and inadvertently become the key witnesses placing Toki and Sayama on the Asakaze Express — their testimony is the foundation of the alibi puzzle and the detectives' first real lead.
